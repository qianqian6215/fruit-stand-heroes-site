A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you jump in.

Is the game really free?

Yes. Fruit Stand Battle is free to play, with no ads or in-app purchases.

Can I play offline?

Yes, the game works offline. Watching the website’s films and visiting community links requires an internet connection.

Where can I play?

On iPhone, iPad, and Android. Visit the App Store or Google Play for device compatibility.

Where is my progress saved?

Your progress is stored on your device. If you need help, contact support before resetting your game.

A video keeps buffering. What can I do?

Films use a smaller playback version to save data. On a slow connection, pause for a moment to let the video load. You can also download the original HD film and watch it after the download finishes.
