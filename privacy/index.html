Fruit Stand Battle

Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026

Information we collect

Fruit Stand Battle does not require an account and does not collect personal information, analytics, advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, or user-generated content. The game does not track you across apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

Game progress, tutorial completion, language choice, and audio settings are stored locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to the developer.

Network use

The app may use standard HTTPS connections to download optional chapter videos. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as an IP address and request time, only as needed to deliver and secure the content. The developer does not use this information for advertising, profiling, or tracking.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the change is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the public support tracker.
