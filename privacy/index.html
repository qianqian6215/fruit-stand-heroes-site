Fruit Stand Battle

Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Information we collect

Fruit Stand Battle does not require an account and does not collect personal information, analytics, advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, or user-generated content. The game does not track you across apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

Game progress, tutorial completion, language choice, and audio settings are stored locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to the developer.

The website remembers your language choice in this browser. This preference is not sent to the developer.

Network use

The app may download optional chapter videos over HTTPS. The website serves its own videos when you choose to play them. On the website, Umami Cloud counts page views and clicks on App Store and Google Play links. It uses no analytics cookies. Umami processes request IP addresses to estimate location and create anonymous sessions, but does not store IP addresses in standard analytics records. We use aggregate results to improve the website, not for advertising or profiling. The website host may process ordinary request information to deliver and secure the site. The game itself has no analytics.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the change is released.
