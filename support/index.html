Quick help

The game has no sound

Check the in-game audio settings and your device volume. On iPhone, also check Silent Mode.

A chapter video does not load

The victory concert is also available on our official website. Tap play to watch with sound. Your saved game progress is not affected if the video is unavailable.

I want to restart my progress

Use the New Game option in Settings. Progress is stored only on your device.

Supported devices

Fruit Stand Battle is available for iPhone, iPad and Android and is played in landscape orientation. Check the App Store or Google Play listing for compatibility with your device.
