Need help?

Open a support request and include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of what happened. Please do not include passwords or other sensitive information.

Quick help

The game has no sound

Check the in-game audio settings, your device volume, and Silent Mode.

A chapter video does not load

Check your connection and try again. Videos fail safely, so you can continue playing if a download is unavailable.

I want to restart my progress

Use the New Game option in Settings. Progress is stored only on your device.

Supported devices

Fruit Stand Heroes supports iPhone and iPad devices running iOS or iPadOS 15.0 or later and is played in landscape orientation.
