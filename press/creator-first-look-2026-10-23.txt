Fruit Stand Battle is a free five-lane tower defense game for Android, iPhone, and iPad. You build a fruit squad, collect Dewdrops, and decide whether to reinforce the lane under pressure now or save for a stronger hero. The game spans Sunny Market, Moonlit Coast, and Cloudcrest, works offline after installation, and has no ads, in-app purchases, or account signup.
