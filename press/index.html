About the game

Fruit Stand Battle is a bright five-lane defense game where a small fruit squad protects its stand from hungry animals and oversized bosses. Each fruit has a readable role: gather Dewdrops, block, heal, control crowds, or attack from range.

The journey moves from a sunny seaside market to a moonlit coast and the windy Cloudcrest chapter. Players choose where to spend resources and which role each lane needs while keeping the whole board in view.
